# R3 — Do not treat sandboxing as sufficient governance

**Rejected capability:** Inferring intent, correctness, review, cost, delivery, or
authority from filesystem isolation — or inferring isolation from a valid SOW.

**Why:** Isolation limits reach. Governance is a different dimension. Both must
appear independently on the receipt and capability manifest.

**Seam:** `sandbox_kind` / `filesystem_isolation` vs SOW status vs
`structured_result_valid` vs `remote_contains`.

**Reopen if:** never as a collapse of the two dimensions; only as additional
probe fields.
