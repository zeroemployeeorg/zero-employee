# Gemini entrypoint

First command: `zeo orient --json`.

Canonical doctrine: `governance/GOVERNANCE.md` (imported from `claude-md/CLAUDE.md`).

Seat type names (`zeo-master`, `zeo-sparring`, `zeo-stream`) are constructors,
not live addresses. Use `zeo relay` to talk to a registered instance.

Gate: `make verify` or `zeo <path>`.
